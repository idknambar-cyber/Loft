How it works

1

Install Loft

Loft is the launcher. It keeps your account, your games and your worlds in one place. Everything runs on your own PC.

2

Make an account

Pick a name and password. It is stored on your PC only, and the same login works on every server.

3

Play or host

Play alone straight away, or press Host a server. Loft makes a link for your friends, no router settings and no server rental.

What is in the game

Survive the nights

Scavenge the docks by day, then hold out until morning while the zombies come.

Build anything

Walls, doors, chests, furnaces, turrets and gardens. Drive abandoned cars.

Up to 20 players

Your worlds, your PC

Questions

Who runs the servers?

You do, when you want to. Nothing here runs all day. When you press Host a server in Loft, your PC runs the world and gives you a link. When you close Loft, the world is saved and the server stops. Your friends only need the link.

Do my friends need to install anything?

No. The link opens the game in their browser. They can also install Loft if they want to host their own worlds.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Loft, your account and singleplayer need no internet. Hosting works for people on the same Wi-Fi. Internet is only needed to install, and for friends who join from outside your home.

Is it safe?

Loft only talks to the internet to download one small helper that makes the join link. Servers never see your Loft password, they get a separate login made from it. Only join servers run by people you trust a bit.
